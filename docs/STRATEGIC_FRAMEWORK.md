## Core Thesis: Organizational Species vs. Category Creation

*(Thesis unchanged from v1.0 — six months of development validated rather than revised it.)*

### The Category Trap

Traditional "blue ocean" strategy assumes creating a **new category** with boundaries contained internally. This approach:
- Claims novelty within closed systems
- Ignores externalities
- Focuses on market capture rather than systemic integration

### Our Position: Organizational Species

We define ourselves as a **new type of organizational species** that:
- **Encompasses categories** rather than being contained by them
- **Cognizant of externalities** — acknowledges interdependencies with broader ecosystems
- **Self-conscious and self-directed** in concept execution
- **B2B focused** with human capacity building as the pivot

### v2.0 Validation Evidence

The Confluence feedback synthesis (see `CONFLUENCE_FEEDBACK_SYNTHESIS.md`) found the last two months of activity **converging on this model without central planning**: the Digital Salon's seven motions, the DACUM occupational analysis work, the mobile deployment gap-closing, and the stance-lens governance requirement each emerged independently and integrate systemically. That convergence is the organizational species behavior the v1.0 thesis predicted.

- Maintains **domain specificity** while enabling cross-pollination

## Pivot: Human Capacity Building

### SCID/DACUM Vocational Training Model

**v2.0 upgrade:** SCID moved from curriculum methodology (v1.0 framing) to **architectural schema layer** — four layers: Competency Schema (ATProto records), Scaffolded Execution (mobile-first PBL), Evidence Capture (field artifacts → B2 → assessment), Credential Issuance (ATProto VCs). Full specification in `SCID_ARCHITECTURE.md`.

Human capacity building serves as:

#### **Both Revenue Center:**
- Training as service offering
- Credential issuance (Verifiable Credentials)
- Guild membership models
- B2B workforce development contracts
- **SCID schema licensing** *(added v2.0)* — the competency schema itself as licensable infrastructure

#### **And Cost Center:**
- Infrastructure R&D investment (~$120K SCID build, Q4 2026–Q3 2027)
- Co-design participant compensation
- Community stewardship overhead
- Pattern library curation

### Integration with Guild Academy

The **Guild Academy Charter** (see `Guild_Academy_Charter.tsx`) operationalizes:
- **UMCES-CGC foundation** — proven technical capacity model at Chesapeake Bay scale
- **Articulation literacy** — AI-assisted domain expertise translation to code
- **Project-based learning (PBL)** — real infrastructure contribution in 6-12 weeks, in three AI-bounded phases (Guided → Assisted → Autonomous)
- **Constitutional bounds** — human sovereignty over AI assistance

**v2.0 gap acknowledged:** mobile deployment was missing from the original Guild Academy architecture (Confluence synthesis warning). The mobile-first AI copilot is now a Guild Academy spec, not an afterthought — field practitioners (vineyard, oyster farm, environmental monitoring) cannot be served by desktop-bound tooling.

## Non-Linear Progression

### Rejection of Speculative Timelines

We **do not timeline this to speculative linear progression** because:

1. **Emergence over planning** — components reveal themselves through development *(demonstrated: the agentic mesh, CEP routing, and stance-lens requirement all emerged from practice, none were in v1.0)*
2. **Feedback-driven iteration** — real-world deployment determines next steps
3. **Partnership-dependent scaling** — B2B relationships mature on their schedules *(observed: GSBF-38 data-sharing MOU stalled 127+ days — a warning, not a failure of the model)*
4. **Technical validation first** — breakthrough comes from demonstrated capability, not projected dates

### Milestones as Inflection Points

Instead of timelines, we track:
- **Deployment completions** — PartyKit Terroir (2026-07-02), Obsidian mesh (July 2026), vault-mcp 8-tool server
- **Guild cohort graduations** (N credentials issued) — *pending first cohort*
- **Pattern Module publications** (M components in library)
- **Partnership activations** (P domains engaged) — South Seattle College collaborative engagement in motion

## B2B Focus: Why Businesses Buy This

### Value Propositions

1. **Workforce Development**
   - Train employees with lived expertise (disability, cultural knowledge, etc.)
   - Reduce time-to-contribution (6-12 weeks vs. 5+ years)
   - AI-assisted onboarding lowers training costs

2. **Infrastructure Validation**
   - Test designs with hardest-case users (tribal sovereignty, wheelchair access)
   - Avoid expensive retrofits post-launch
   - Regulatory compliance through co-design

3. **IP & Credential Portability**
   - Verifiable Credentials for employee skills
   - Pattern Modules reusable across projects
   - Knowledge transfer without platform lock-in

4. **Economic Sovereignty**
   - Transparent revenue splits (70/20/10 models)
   - Community wealth building
   - Brand differentiation through equitable practices

### v2.0 Known Gap: Multi-Stance Governance

Multi-stakeholder B2B (the GUEST/OPERATOR/VENUE/PROTOCOL stance lens) is a **governance architecture requirement identified across all surfaces** but not yet built (ENG-389, targeted Q4 2026). Until it ships, stance-differentiated sales collateral and multi-stakeholder contracts carry design risk. Named here so it is priced into engagements, not discovered inside them.
