## II. Better NSF Program Fits

### A. ATE: Advanced Technological Education (Primary NSF Path)

**Program URL:** https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/ate-advanced-technological-education

**Why This Fits Better:**

| SCiPE (Wrong Fit) | ATE (Right Fit) |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Research cyberinfrastructure professionals | **Skilled technical workforce** (exactly Guild Academy) |
| 4-year research universities | **Community colleges + industry partnerships** (Walla Walla CC, hospitality partners) |
| Computational science support roles | **Applied technology fields** (viticulture sensors, AR accessibility, data governance) |
| National scope (3-4 institutions) | **Regional/industry-specific** (Pacific Northwest wine corridor, USVI hospitality) |

**ATE Program Priorities:**
1. **Technician education** — Guild Academy's SCID competencies (Movement Documentation Technologist, Cultural Data Steward)
2. **Two-year colleges** — Walla Walla Community College, USVI community colleges
3. **Industry partnerships** — Sonesta (hospitality), Verizon (telecom infrastructure), wineries (viticulture)
4. **Curriculum/materials development** — SCID competency schemas, mobile-first training materials
5. **Professional development for educators** — Training instructors to use AI copilot + SCID assessment

**Grant Types:**
- **ATE Projects ($225K-$900K over 3 years)** — Develop, implement, evaluate new technician education approaches
- **ATE Centers ($7.5M-10M over 5 years)** — National/regional resource for specific technology area (aspirational, not Year 1)

**Application Strategy:**

**Lead Institution:** Walla Walla Community College (or USVI community college)
- **NSF requires educational institution as PI** — Guild Academy/Stadium Soundwave is **industry partner**, not lead applicant
- **Role:** Curriculum design partner (SCID schemas), technology provider (mobile AI copilot), workforce outcome evaluator

**Project Framing:**
> "Developing a competency-based technician education model for emerging fields (cultural technology, data sovereignty, experiential production) where traditional 4-year degrees don't exist but industry demand is growing. Leverages AI-assisted learning to reduce time-to-competency (12 weeks vs. 2-5 years) while maintaining rigor through evidence-based assessment."

**Competitive Advantages:**
- **Mobile-first deployment** (field practitioners, not lab-bound)
- **Industry co-designed** (Sonesta, wineries validate competencies, hire graduates)
- **Portable credentials** (ATProto VCs, not platform-locked certificates)
- **AI-bounded scaffolding** (addresses "AI in education" NSF priority without replacing instructors)

**Funding Potential:**
- **ATE Project (3 years):** $600K-900K
  - Year 1: Curriculum development (3 SCID competency schemas) + mobile UI prototype
  - Year 2: Pilot cohort (20 students, Walla Walla CC + USVI)
  - Year 3: Evaluation + dissemination (publish schemas for other colleges)

**Timeline:**
- **Submission deadline:** NSF ATE has annual deadline (typically October-November)
- **Preparation:** 3-4 months (requires institutional buy-in from community college)
- **Decision:** 6-9 months from submission
- **Earliest funding:** 12-15 months from now (Q4 2027 realistic start)

**Next Actions:**

### B. NSF Regional Innovation Engines (Direct Influence Path)

**Program URL:** https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/regional-innovation-engines

**Why This Matters:**

Regional Innovation Engines (RIE) is **$2.4B, 10-year NSF initiative** (launched 2022, first awards 2023) to build **regional innovation ecosystems** around specific technology areas.

**Key Difference from ATE:**
- **ATE = education/workforce focus** (technician training, curriculum)
- **RIE = ecosystem focus** (startups, anchor institutions, workforce, infrastructure together)

**RIE Structure:**
- **Engines ($160M over 10 years)** — Large regional consortia (universities, companies, economic development orgs)
- **Engine Development Awards ($1M over 2 years)** — Planning grants to build coalition, define use case

**Guild Academy's RIE Fit:**

**We're not leading an RIE** (requires anchor research university + regional coalition). **We're a spoke in an existing or forming Engine.**

**Potential RIE Themes We Align With:**
1. **AI + Cultural Heritage** (AI-generated content, ethical datasets, Indigenous data sovereignty)
2. **Tourism/Hospitality Innovation** (experiential tech, accessibility, workforce development)
3. **Island/Coastal Resilience** (USVI, Hawaii, Pacific territories as testbeds for climate-adaptive infrastructure)

**Active RIE Awards to Monitor:**

| Engine | Region | Focus Area | Our Entry Point |
|--------|--------|------------|-----------------|
| **Inclusive Growth Engine** (multiple awarded) | Various regions | Economic inclusion, workforce equity | SCID vocational training as inclusive workforce model |
| **Pacific Northwest Engine** (if one exists/forms) | WA, OR | Could include: agriculture tech, hospitality innovation, Indigenous data sovereignty | Walla Walla CC viticulture, tribal partnerships |
| **Caribbean/USVI Engine** (if one forms) | USVI, PR, territories | Island resilience, tourism innovation, digital equity | Sound Clash as cultural + workforce infrastructure |

**Strategy:**
- **Monitor NSF RIE announcements** (new Engines awarded on 2-year cycles)
- **Position Guild Academy as workforce development partner** when Pacific Northwest or Caribbean/USVI Engine forms
- **Leverage ATE funding as proof point** ("We already trained 50 technologists via NSF ATE; we're the workforce arm of this Engine")

**Funding Potential:**
- **Not direct funding to us** (flows to Engine lead)
- **But:** Being a named partner in $160M Engine = credibility, co-funding opportunities, regional visibility

**Timeline:**
- **RIE is long-term play** (not 2027 revenue)
- **Monitor for 2026-2027 new Engine announcements**
- **Engage when coalition-building phase starts** (before full proposal)
